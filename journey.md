Here on this page I am going to explain all that I have done so far being into Programming and building stuff for a couple of year and also talk about my experience in AI research.

I got into programming for the first time back when I was in class 9, We had C programming as a part of our computer science curriculum in school, we had HTML too in class 8 but that didn't excite me much like C programming did (Those TurboC programming days!). That's where I got very curious about programming in general and started getting deeper into it. My aunt is a PhD in Artificial Intelligence, back when I was in class 9 she was doing her Master's as far as I remember and also had C programming books which she used to study from during her bachelor's. I started studying and exploring C programming from her books, implementing the exercise questions in the book by myself and getting happier as I got deeper.

Later she suggested me to learn Python and also explore the field of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning because she herself was into AI and ML and told me that it's a really interesting domain to explore and learn and soon going to be the future. I agreed to her suggestion and now looking back I feel like agreeing to all her suggestions and getting into this field were the best decisions I made in my life. 

I started learning Python Programming from this book named Core Python Programming by R. Nageshwara Rao, my aunt gave me as a gift. I went through one chapter daily, did assignments and followed what my aunt said me to do. In a month, I started writing my own GUIs, writing games with Pygame, etc. Soon after learning Python, I got into Machine Learning, watched all the machine learning and deep learning courses by Andrew Ng. I wanted to get deeper but after I finished my class 10, I needed to gear up and study for JEE (Joint Entrance Exam) and kept coding and ML aside for a while to focus on entrance exam prep.
